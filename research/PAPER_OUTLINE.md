# Paper outline
**Reliability-Aware Multimodal Sensor Fusion for Explainable UAV Classification under Environmental and Sensor Uncertainty**
- Motivation and civilian airspace-safety scope
- Related work: multimodal fusion, missing-modality learning, calibration
- Data provenance, licenses, privacy and split protocol
- Baselines: early fusion, late fusion, reliability-aware fusion
- Evaluation: macro-F1, balanced accuracy, Brier score, ECE, modality dropout
- Ablations, confidence intervals, limitations, ethics and reproducibility
